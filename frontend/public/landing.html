How it works

01

Snap

Photograph the front, the reverse, and a close edge or resist line.

02

Check

A DINOv2 vision model scores genuine hand-dyed vs factory print, or says "uncertain" instead of guessing.

03

Trust

Get reasons, find artisans on the map, and give verified pieces a QR passport.

Questions

Is this a certificate?

No. It's a decision aid with a confidence score, and it abstains when unsure.

Where did the data come from?

Our teammate photographed genuine pieces at a workshop in Ibadan, alongside factory prints from the market.

What's next?

More artisans and pieces, naira price guidance, and partnerships with cooperatives.
